Monitoring systems during Hajj

Real-time visibility into devices and network infrastructure at Mina Emergency Hospital during Hajj.

  • IoT
  • Network monitoring
  • Operations dashboards

The problem

During Hajj, the operations team needed a live view of devices and network infrastructure, with little time to discover issues.

What I built

I built IoT monitoring systems to track devices and network infrastructure and give the operations team a live view of their status.

The outcome

The monitoring system ran without downtime during the season. I presented the work to a Ministry of Health official.

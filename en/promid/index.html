03 /Digital product

PROMID

A digital book of 250 structured prompts for practical AI use at work and in everyday life.

  • Product design
  • Arabic content
  • Interactive PDF

The problem

A vague request leaves an AI tool guessing. I wanted readers to have a starting point they could use immediately and tailor to their needs.

What I built

I organized 250 prompts into five sections covering daily life, work, marketing, money, and creative projects. Readers pick a prompt from the index, fill in the fields, and review a sample result.

An example from the book

The day planner asks for fixed appointments, tasks, the top priority, working hours, and energy level before proposing a schedule.

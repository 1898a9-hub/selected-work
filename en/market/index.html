04 /Commerce automation

Price comparison engine

A comparison engine for Turkish marketplaces that checks product identity and specs before comparing prices.

  • Python
  • Product matching
  • Commerce data

The problem

An offer may look cheaper because it has less storage or lists an accessory instead of the device. The comparison starts with product identity.

What I built

I collected offers from Amazon Türkiye, Trendyol, and Hepsiburada. The engine checks model numbers and specs such as storage, size, and pack quantity, and rejects accessories and mismatches.

What I tested

I ran a dry run for a basket of six household products. The basket met the matching and savings criteria in that test.
